Frequently Asked Questions

What is a circle?

A circle is a group of people who care about each other. It could be your family, fitness partners, recovery group, or any group you want to stay connected with. Each circle has scheduled check-ins, AI-powered nudges, and alerts.

How do check-ins work?

Circle members receive daily check-in questions at scheduled times (e.g., "Did Mom take her medicine?"). They respond with a simple yes/no or a short answer. If someone misses a check-in, the circle is notified.

Does the AI contact my family members?

No. HeartKnock's AI only nudges you to take action. It will never call, text, or message your circle members directly. You're always in control.

Is my health data safe?

Yes. Health data is processed on your device. Only daily aggregated summaries (like average step count) are shared with your circle — never raw data. You can revoke access anytime in Settings.

What is the Vault?

The Vault is an encrypted storage space for important documents, messages, and files. Items you set to deliver automatically are encrypted too; our server can unlock them only to deliver them. You can set a "dead man's switch" — if you don't check in for a set number of days, your Vault contents are delivered to your circle.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Delete Account. This initiates a complete data deletion request. All your data, including circle memberships, check-in history, vault contents, and health data, will be permanently deleted within 30 days.

Free vs Pro — what do I get?

Free: 1 circle, 2 members, 3 nudges/day, check-ins, feed, daily brief, AI summaries, and panic button.

Pro: Unlimited circles and members, TendAI chat, vault, health data sharing, and unlimited nudges.
